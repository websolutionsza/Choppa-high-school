About Choppa School

Located in the heart of Riverside, Gauteng, Choppa School has been a pillar of academic excellence and community development for over four decades. We are dedicated to nurturing the potential of every learner.

Our Story

A tradition of learning, leadership, and belonging.

Founded in 1978, Choppa School began as a small community initiative with just 120 learners. Today, we are proud to educate over 1,240 learners from Grade 8 to Grade 12, offering a diverse curriculum that balances rigorous academics with vibrant sports and cultural programs.

Our experienced educators are committed to creating a supportive environment where students are challenged to think critically, act responsibly, and contribute meaningfully to society. We believe that every child has the right to a high-quality education, and we work tirelessly to ensure our learners are equipped for the challenges of tomorrow.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution of academic excellence, empowering learners to become innovative, compassionate, and responsible citizens who are prepared to make a meaningful impact in an ever-changing global society.

Our Mission

Choppa School is committed to providing a holistic, high-quality education that nurtures the intellectual, physical, and emotional growth of every learner. We strive to create an inclusive environment that fosters critical thinking, creativity, and a lifelong love for learning.

What We Stand For

School Values

Our core values guide our daily interactions, shape our culture, and define the character we instill in our learners.

Excellence

We pursue the highest standards in teaching, learning, and personal conduct, encouraging every learner to achieve their personal best.

Integrity

We act with honesty, fairness, and respect, taking responsibility for our actions and fostering a culture of trust.

Compassion

We treat others with empathy and kindness, celebrating diversity and supporting one another as a unified school community.

Service

We actively contribute to our community, understanding that true leadership involves serving others and making a positive difference.

Innovation

We embrace creative thinking and modern methodologies, preparing our learners to navigate and shape the future.

Discipline

We maintain a structured and safe environment where self-discipline and respect for rules are fundamental to personal growth.

Academic Information

Curriculum & Extracurriculars

We offer a comprehensive curriculum aligned with the South African National Senior Certificate (NSC), supplemented by a wide range of activities to ensure holistic development.

Enquire about Admissions

Senior Phase (Grades 8 & 9)

In the Senior Phase, learners build a strong foundation across essential learning areas, preparing them for the Further Education and Training phase. Our focus is on developing critical thinking and collaborative problem-solving skills.

  • English Home Language
  • Mathematics & Natural Sciences
  • Social Sciences & Technology
  • Economic & Management Sciences
  • Life Orientation & Creative Arts

FET Phase (Grades 10 to 12)

In the FET phase, learners specialize in subjects that align with their career aspirations. Our dedicated educators provide rigorous preparation for the final NSC examinations, ensuring our learners are well-prepared for tertiary education.

  • Physical Sciences & Life Sciences
  • Accounting & Business Studies
  • Geography & History
  • Computer Applications Technology (CAT)
  • Visual Arts & Dramatic Arts

Extracurricular Activities

Education extends beyond the classroom. We offer a diverse range of sports and cultural activities designed to develop teamwork, leadership, and personal wellness. Every learner is encouraged to participate in at least one sport and one cultural activity per year.

  • Sports: Football, Basketball, Netball, Rugby, Athletics
  • Arts: Marimba Band, Choir, Drama, Visual Arts
  • Clubs: Debate, Science Expo, Chess, Eco-Club
